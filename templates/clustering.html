Elbow Method for Optimal k
Inertia Curve

Mathematical Justification: The Within-Cluster Sum of Squares (Inertia) was evaluated for $k \in [2, 8]$. The distinctive "elbow" inflection occurs at $k = 3$, beyond which marginal variance reduction diminishes.

Differentiated Teacher & Mentor Action Strategy
High Performers Group

Self-motivated, high attendance (>78%), and high internal marks.

  • Encourage competitive coding and research papers.
  • Assign leadership roles in collaborative group assignments.
  • Guidance for GATE / GRE / Industry internships.
Moderate Performers Group

Capable students with periodic slips in attendance or quiz scores.

  • Structured weekly assignment deadlines.
  • Peer study buddies to reinforce challenging syllabus modules.
  • Mid-semester check-in before final university examinations.
At-Risk Segment

High detention risk with critical gaps across multiple subjects.

  • Immediate mentor counseling and parent notification.
  • Mandatory remedial / tutorial sessions.
  • Daily attendance tracking and milestone monitoring.

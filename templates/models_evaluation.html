Challenge & Corrective Action: SMOTE Class Balancing
Review Feedback #10 Addressed

Real-world classroom datasets have few failing students (~17% at-risk), which causes standard algorithms to suffer from high false-negative rates (failing to detect weak students early).

Corrective Action Implemented: Applied SMOTE (Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique) combined with 5-fold stratified cross-validation. This generated synthetic feature combinations for at-risk cases, dramatically improving the model's ability to catch weak students before semester finals.
